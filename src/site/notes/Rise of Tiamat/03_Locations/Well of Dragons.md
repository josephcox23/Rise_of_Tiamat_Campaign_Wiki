# Well of Dragons

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"The sky above the jagged volcanic crater burns the color of bruised purple and spilled blood, echoing with the deafening roar of a thousand dragon wings."*

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The air is thick with choking sulfur, volcanic ash, and the oppressive heat of bubbling magma. The sky is perpetually choked by thick, roiling storm clouds and soaring chromatic dragons, punctuated by the relentless sounds of clashing armies, monstrous roars, and archaic chanting.
* **Environment & Geography:** A massive, dormant volcanic caldera surrounded by jagged mountain peaks. Its subterranean depths house vast subterranean chambers and a central temple built to facilitate the summoning of Tiamat.
* **Public Perception:** Known across the Sword Coast as the terrifying epicenter of the Cult of the Dragon's apocalyptic conspiracy—the forbidden dead-zone where the fate of the mortal world will be decided.
