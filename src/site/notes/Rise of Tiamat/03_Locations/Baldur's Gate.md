# Baldur’s Gate

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"Where coin rules the upper streets and blood runs in the outer wards, Baldur’s Gate stands as a fortress of high stone and unyielding ambition."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[baldurs_gate_map.png]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** The Upper City, The Lower City, The Outer Wards, The Harbor.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The sharp tang of sea salt and river mud mingling with the heavy clang of smithing hammers, the tramping of booted mercenary patrols, and the sharp clinking of heavy coin purses.
* **Environment & Geography:** A massive, walled metropolis built into a steep river bluff along the River Chionthar, divided rigidly into the wealthy Upper City, the bustling and densely packed Lower City, and the sprawling, chaotic Outer Wards.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed across the Sword Coast as a formidable, pragmatic mercantile powerhouse governed by iron-fisted law and mercenary muscle, though fiercely protective of its trade monopolies.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[The Upper City\|The Upper City]]:** *The pristine, walled sanctuary of patrician nobles and high-ranking merchants guarded by elite watchmen.*
2. **[[The Lower City & Harbor\|The Lower City & Harbor]]:** *The sprawling commercial crescent surrounding the port where ships unload wealth and goods from across the Sea of Swords.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Naval trade goods, arms, armor, mercenary contracts, raw minerals, and luxury imports from across Faerûn.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** Gold standard coinage backed by the city's powerful banking houses and merchant guilds.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Uldar Ravengard\|Uldar Ravengard]]:** *Grand Duke & Marshal of the Flaming Fist* — Supreme military commander coordinating the city's defense and council coalition efforts.
* **Mercenary Captains & Guildmasters:** Influential figures managing trade houses and security forces across the wards.
