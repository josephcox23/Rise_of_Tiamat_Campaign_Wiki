# Waterdeep

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"There is no gold like the gold of Waterdeep, and no city quite like the City of Splendors."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[Map of Waterdeep.webp]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** The Open Lord's Palace, Castle Ward, The Trades Ward, The Dock Ward.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The chiming of high clocktowers, the rich aroma of merchant spices mingling with sea salt, the bustling hum of thousands of traders from every corner of Faerûn, and the glittering spires catching the northern sun.
* **Environment & Geography:** A sprawling, magnificent coastal metropolis built around a deep-water harbor, structured into distinct wards ranging from opulent noble villas to gritty dockside alleyways.
* **Public Perception:** Universally recognized across the Sword Coast as the pinnacle of civilization, trade, culture, and political leadership—the heart where the free realms unite against darkness.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[Castle Ward & Palace\|Castle Ward & Palace]]:** *The fortified administrative center housing the Open Lord's Palace, elite guard garrisons, and council chambers.*
2. **[[The Trades & Dock Wards\|The Trades & Dock Wards]]:** *Vibrant commercial hubs where merchants, guilds, and shipping enterprises drive the city's immense economy.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Luxury goods, masterwork crafts, magical items, financial services, and maritime trade from across Faerûn.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** Dragons, Taela, Shards, and Harbor gold standards backed by the most powerful banking houses on the continent.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Laeral Silverhand\|Laeral Silverhand]]:** *Open Lord of Waterdeep* — Legendary archmage and political leader presiding over the Council of Waterdeep.
