# Darkhold

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"Carved into the sheer black cliffs of the Far Hills, Darkhold looms like a jagged shadow against the sky—a grim bastion where secrets are bought, sold, and enforced with cold steel."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[darkhold_map.png]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** The Upper Keep, The Subterranean Dungeons, The Shadow Vaults.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The chilling mountain winds howling through jagged battlements, the echoing clank of armored guards in dark stone corridors, and the sharp scent of old parchment and hidden poisons.
* **Environment & Geography:** A massive, ancient stone fortress built directly into a towering precipice in the Far Hills, surrounded by treacherous mountain passes and heavily guarded approaches.
* **Public Perception:** Feared across the Sword Coast as the impenetrable mountain stronghold and clandestine headquarters of the Zhentarim (the Black Network)—a forbidden place where criminals, mercenaries, and shadow brokers pull the strings of regional commerce.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[The Upper Bastion\|The Upper Bastion]]:** *The heavily fortified command center and living quarters for high-ranking Zhentarim inner circle members.*
2. **[[The Shadow Vaults & Dungeons\|The Shadow Vaults & Dungeons]]:** *Deep, subterranean chambers used to store illicit contraband, poison supplies, and interrogated prisoners.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Mercenary contracts, smuggled contraband, poison caches, shadow intelligence, and black-market trade monopolies.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** High-value bullion, untraceable gem currency, and strict ledger-backed credit notes.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **[[Manshoon\|Manshoon]]** *(or ruling Zhentarim Lords):* *Shadow Master* — Supreme leadership directing the Black Network's continental operations.
* **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Rian Nightshade\|Rian Nightshade]]:** *Zhentarim Operative & Information Broker* — Manages shadow logistics and network intelligence.
