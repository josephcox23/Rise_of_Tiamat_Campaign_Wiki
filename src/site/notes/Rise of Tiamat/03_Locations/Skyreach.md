# Skyreach Castle

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"A majestic, colossal mass of carved ice and stone drifting silently through the clouds, bearing the stolen riches and dark secrets of the Cult of the Dragon."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[skyreach_castle_map.png]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** The Ice Tunnels, The Dragon Stables, The Upper Citadel Spire.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The howling roar of high-altitude winds, the blinding glare of sunlight off massive carved ice ramparts, the heavy scent of ozone and dragon scales, and the creaking of ancient magic keeping the colossal fortress aloft.
* **Environment & Geography:** An awe-inspiring floating castle carved from enchanted ice and solid stone, capable of drifting across the skies of the Sword Coast under the command of cloud giant allies and cult leaders.
* **Public Perception:** Known to high-level adventurers and faction leaders as the mobile logistics and treasure-hauling powerhouse of the Cult of the Dragon, transporting stolen gold and artifact masks across the North.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[The Ice Tunnels & Barracks\|The Ice Tunnels & Barracks]]:** *Chilling subterranean chambers housing cult guards, ogres, and red wizard attendants.*
2. **[[The Upper Citadel Spire\|The Upper Citadel Spire]]:** *Ornate staves, command chambers, and the soaring docking towers used by dragon riders and wyrmspeakers.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Stolen gold hoards, tribute chests from across the Sword Coast, magical scrolls, and dragon egg cargo.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** None; operated entirely through cult fanaticism, giant alliances, and coercive military might.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **Wyrmspeaker Rezmir:** *Black Wyrmspeaker* — Former commander of the castle's treasury and operations.
* **Talis the White:** *Former Cult Contender* — Provided crucial boarding schedules and banner passwords before her demise.
