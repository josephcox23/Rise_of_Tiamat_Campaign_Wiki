# Silverymoon

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"Where silver spires pierce the northern sky and the magic of the weave flows like water, Silverymoon stands as a sanctuary of art, learning, and peace."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[silverymoon_map.png]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** The Moonbridge, The Vault of the Sages, The High Palace.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The gentle hum of ambient abjuration magic, the soft gleam of moonlit silver spires, the strumming of bardic lutes in pristine courtyards, and the crisp, clean air of the northern river valley.
* **Environment & Geography:** A breathtaking, meticulously planned magical metropolis spanning both banks of the River Rauvin, connected by the legendary, insubstantial Moonbridge of pure force.
* **Public Perception:** Revered across Faerûn as the cultural and magical capital of the North—a beacon of enlightenment, racial harmony, and high scholarship.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[The Moonbridge\|The Moonbridge]]:** *A magnificent, shimmering bridge of magical force that spans the River Rauvin without physical piers.*
2. **[[The Vault of the Sages\|The Vault of the Sages]]:** *An immense, heavily warded library and repository of ancient lore, spells, and historical archives.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Fine arts, magical scrolls, potions, scholarly treatises, crafted silverwork, and advanced magical research.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** Silver Moons and standard northern coinage backed by the city's powerful merchant and academic guilds.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Taern Hornblade\|Taern Hornblade]]:** *High Mage of Silverymoon* — Venerable archmage and council leader providing immense magical wisdom and defensive strategy.
* **Archmages and Master Scholars:** Leaders of the Spellguard and the Vault of the Sages.
