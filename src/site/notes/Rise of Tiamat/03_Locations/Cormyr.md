# Cormyr

> [!quote] Atmosphere / Local Proverb
> *"Beneath the Purple Dragon banner, knightly honor and royal steel keep the wild frontier at bay."*

## Cartography & Maps

* **Regional/City Map:** `![[cormyr_map.png]]`
* **Key Tactical Layouts:** Suzail Royal Palace, The King's Forest, Stonewall Border Garrisons.

## Overview & Description

* **Sensory Details:** The fluttering of purple dragon pennants in crisp forest breezes, the ringing of iron-shod hooves on royal stone roads, and the proud, stern hymns of traveling paladin escorts.
* **Environment & Geography:** A prosperous, heavily forested feudal realm characterized by rolling farmlands, deep ancient woodlands (the King's Forest), and fortified mountain passes guarded by elite Purple Dragon knights.
* **Public Perception:** Revered across Faerûn as a bastion of chivalry, law, and royal order, fiercely protected by its sovereign monarchs and knightly defenders.

## Key Points of Interest & Districts

1. **[[Suzail\|Suzail]]:** *The glittering royal capital and primary port along the Dragonmere, housing the royal palace and Purple Dragon command.*
2. **[[The King's Forest\|The King's Forest]]:** *An immense, royal-protected woodland patrolled by expert rangers and elite knights.*

## Local Economy & Resources

* **Primary Exports / Imports:** Fine timber, agriculture, masterwork steel weapons, suits of armor, and royal wines.
* **Trade Hubs & Currency:** Royal Golden Lions and Silver falcons minted directly by the Crown.

## Notable Residents & NPCs

* **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Sir Isteval\|Sir Isteval]]:** *Renowned Knight-Paladin* — A celebrated hero of Cormyr embodying the realm's highest ideals on the Council of Waterdeep.
* **Royal Wardens & Knight Commanders:** Leaders managing border garrisons and royal security forces.
