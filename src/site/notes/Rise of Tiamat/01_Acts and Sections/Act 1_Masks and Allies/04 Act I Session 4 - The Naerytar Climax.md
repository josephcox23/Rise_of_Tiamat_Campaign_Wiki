## Recap

> Stepping off the High Road and plunging into the sulfurous haze of the Mere of Dead Men, your journey was paved with the quiet weight of ancient secrets and fractured alliances. Along the marsh’s edge, you gathered the scattered threads of regional factions’ claiming the subtle network of the Harpers, the unyielding roar of the Lion’s Battle Horn, and the hidden cache of the Black Network.
> Through cleverness and resolve, you solved the ancient mechanisms of the Sunken Gate, forcing open a path through the brackish, rot-choked waterways of the inner swamp. But the Mere yields nothing easily. Out of the stagnant mist came Snapjaw and his lizardfolk hunters’ blades drawn, eyes burning with a desperate, suppressed hatred for the bullywug tyrants serving Wyrmspeaker Rezmir.
> In the quiet rustle of the reeds, a pact was struck in blood. Smuggled through a rusted, submerged drainage grate beneath Castle Naerytar, you slipped into the wet underbelly of the fortress like ghosts. Guided by silence, you traced the foul chanting deep into the flooded mud caves, catching the bullywug shaman Pharblex Spatterdock completely unawares. In a swift, brutal flurry of steel and magic, Pharblex and his lieutenants were torn down, bringing their cruelty to a sudden, violent end.
> And that... is where we bring our eyes back to the table.
> The air in the cavern is thick with the heavy stench of sulfur, damp earth, and fresh blood. The bloated corpse of Pharblex lies still upon the mud at your feet. And waiting tensely in the shadows of the cave exit, Snapjaw watches you... waiting to see if you carry the trophy that will spark a revolution.

# Session 4: The Naerytar Climax

## Scene 1: Sparking the Mutiny

> You step into the shadow of the cave entrance, holding high the severed head and crocodile-scale shield of Pharblex Spatterdock. Snapjaw’s slitted eyes widen, reflecting the dim torchlight. He slowly lowers his spear, opens his jaws, and lets out a low, vibrating war-hiss that rattles the mud beneath your boots. 
> Across the courtyard, the hiss is answered by dozens of throats. The tension snaps. Enslaved lizardfolk rise up with iron tools and bare claws, throwing bullywugs from the parapets. Cultists shriek orders that are immediately swallowed by the roar of rebellion. Snapjaw points his blade toward the central stone tower. "The lizardfolk hold the courtyard! The elf-lord burns his papers in the high tower—go now, before the secrets turn to ash!"

### Skill Challenge: The Courtyard Crossfire

To reach the spiral stairs of the Command Tower through the chaotic melee, the party must navigate the crossfire. 
* **Group Check:** DC 12 Group Athletics or Stealth check. 
* **Success:** The party weave through the riot unscathed and breach the tower door cleanly. 
* **Failure:** The party still reach the tower, but stray crossfire or flying debris inflicts 1d6 slashing damage on each character before they reach the stairs.

## Scene 2: The Command Tower Duel

> You burst onto the top floor of the keep into the pristine, elven-built Command Quarters. The air is thick with acrid black smoke. At the far end of the room, Elf-Lord Dralmorrer Borngray stands beside a massive oak desk, frantically sweeping armfuls of maps and leather-bound ledgers directly into a roaring stone hearth. 
> He spins toward you, drawing a pristine, poison-coated rapier with a sneer. "Miserable intruders... you will burn with the rest of my work!"

> [!note] Lair Hazard: Spreading Inferno & Heavy Smoke
> * **Initiative Count 20:** Every round on Initiative Count 20, fire spreads to 2 adjacent floor squares. 
> * **Fire Damage:** Any creature starting its turn in or moving through a burning square takes 1d6 fire damage. 
>* **Heavy Smoke:** Any character ending their turn inside the room must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn due to coughing and stinging eyes.

> [! Notes] Interactions: Salvaging the Burning Documents

Players can use their action or object interaction to snatch burning documents from the hearth before they turn to ash. 

>* **The Check:** A character adjacent to the hearth can make a **DC 13 Sleight of Hand or Dexterity check**.
>* **On a Failure:** The character snatches a singed page but takes 1d6 fire damage from the flames.

## Scene 3: The Awakening of the Verdant Fang

> As Elf-Lord Borngray falls, his weight collapses backward against a central support beam. Combined with the fires and the structural damage from the riot below, the ancient stone gives way with a deafening crack. The ancient masonry groans, and a massive section of the wall splits open with a deafening crack, choking the chamber with stone dust. 
> The dust clears to reveal a jagged fissure where the solid stone wall once stood. Inside, the stagnant air of the castle is replaced by the overwhelming, heavy scent of damp earth, crushed pine, and ancient ozone. The foundations here don't rest on stone, but on the massive, pulsing root systems of the colossal banyan tree. 
> Entwined tightly within a vice-like grip of thick, living roots is a pulsing hum of emerald energy. It is exactly as it appeared in Logan's vision: a shattered, brilliant emerald spearhead attached to a living wooden haft. The roots are actively squeezing, constricting around it as if trying to swallow the relic back into the earth. The weapon is calling out, its vibration a low, desperate thrum in the soles of your boots. 

### The Trial of the Primal Will

* **Logan's Action:** Logan must step forward to claim the relic.  He can automatically pacify the roots by expending a **2nd-level or higher spell slot** to channel nature magic, or succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom (Nature or Athletics) check**.
* Success: The roots unravel like flexing fingers, releasing the weapon cleanly.
* **Failure:** The roots lash out defensively, dealing **2d6 bludgeoning damage** before the primal energy forces them open. 

As the weapon is pulled free, the shattered emerald shards instantly snap together, seamlessly fusing into a pristine, glowing green spearhead.

### The Aftermath of the Tower Duel

> With the Verdant Fang secured in hand, the emerald light pulsing gently against your palm, you step back through the settling dust of the broken banyan chamber into the Command Quarters. 
> The roaring fire that threatened to consume the room has been smothered—several lizardfolk warriors are stamping out the remaining embers under heavy, wet hides and buckets of swamp water. Beyond the shattered windows, the frantic sounds of screams and steel have given way to a deep, rhythmic thumping: dozens of lizardfolk beating the butts of their spears against the stone parapets in victory. The bullywugs have been driven into the swamp, and the remaining cultists lie bound or routed. 

> Snapjaw steps through the doorway, his weapon dripped in blood and his scales streaked with soot.  He pauses, his slitted eyes fixing on the glowing green spear in Logan's grip with a look of profound, quiet awe. 
> Snapjaw lowers his head in a brief gesture of deep respect before looking back up at the party. 
> "The castle is ours, stone-walkers. The swamp-tyrants are broken. But the half-dragon, Rezmir, is not among the dead. Before the fighting reached the tower, she fled into the deep cellar, down where the muck gives way to cold, pale stone. There is a magic ring of frozen light hidden beneath our feet. She stepped into the cold wind and vanished."
> 
> He turns and gestures for you to follow, leading you down the central spiral staircase to the ground level of the keep. Passing through the quieted halls, he stops in a secluded back alcove and kicks aside a heavy, mud-stained rug. Beneath it lies a heavy iron-bound trapdoor set into the floor. 
> Snapjaw hurls the door open, revealing a flight of pristine, white marble steps descending into the cool darkness. "We keep watch above. Your quarry waits beyond the frost.

## Scene 4: The Sub-Cellar Vault & Act I Finale

> Guided down the heavy trapdoor by Snapjaw, the ambient noise of the victory cheers above dies away into absolute silence. Beneath the rotting wood and wet stone lies a vaulted cellar of smooth, unweathered white marble. The humidity, muck, and rot disappear entirely, replaced by air that tastes like crisp, thin, frozen mountain air. 
> In the center of the room, a massive circular ring of silver runes flares with a cold, pale blue light. On a stone pedestal next to the gate sits a heavy leather ledger seemingly belonging to Wyrmspeaker Rezmir.

### The Will of the Black Dragon Mask

* **Save Trigger:** The character carrying the true Black Dragon Mask must make a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw**.
* **Failure:** A terrifying chorus of five overlapping draconic voices whispers into their mind, urging them to hide their newly acquired 1,500 gp from the party and keep the mask hidden from the Council of Waterdeep.

### Examining the Ledger & Act I Exit

> The final entry in the leather binder is written in a frantic, scratching hand: 
> 
> *"Borngray, the thieves have slipped into the marsh with the Black Mask. The Hoard is secure at the lodge, but my position is compromised if I do not return with the relic. I am stepping through the gate to secure the perimeter on the other side. Once you have dispatched with the interlopers, gather the mask they carry and speak the phrase 'Drako' while stepping into the ring to trace the link. Do not fail me elf, or the consequences will be dire."*
> 
> You stand over the glowing silver ring as the frosty air howls gently from its center. You have broken the cult's grip on the swamp, freed an army, and claimed a legendary weapon. As you speak the passkey and step into the pale blue light, close your eyes, take a breath, and congratulate yourselves: **You are now Level 10!**
