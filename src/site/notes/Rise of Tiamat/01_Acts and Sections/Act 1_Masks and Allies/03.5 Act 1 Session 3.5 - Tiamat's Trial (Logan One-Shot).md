## The Catalyst: The Split-Second Blackout

## Phase 1: The Endless Bog

### Description

Logan lands softly in an infinite, nightmare version of the Mere of Dead Men. 

> [!danger] **The Sulfur Marsh**
> The darkness breaks. You stand in an endless, misty bog under a sky the color of a bruised, purple vein. Ankle-deep water stretches out infinitely in every direction, completely flat and undisturbed. The air is thick, choking, and reeks heavily of rotting eggs and sulfur. 
> 
> You see formless shapes to begin to emerge from the ground. The shadows begin to coalesce into two, three, four creatures. Each is faceless but you recognize the forms of the Lizardfolk and Bullywug’s you fought in the Mere. They act without fear or regard for their safety, lunging at you instantly. 

### Encounter: The Shambling Husks

* **Enemies:** 6x **Faceless Lizardfolk Shadows**, 6x **Faceless Bullywug Shadows**
* **Mechanics (Pop-and-Drop):** These are minion-style enemies. They have their standard armor class, but **any successful hit or damaging spell disperses them instantly** (1–2 hits max to clear the field). They rise seamlessly from the sulfur water.
* **The Combat Flow:** They try to swamp Logan by sheer numbers, clawing at his legs.

```encounter
name: 3.5 Tiamat's Trial - 1 (three rounds)
players:
  - Logan
creatures:
  - "1": Faceless Lizardfolk Shadow
  - "1": Faceless Lizardfolk Shadow
  - "1": Faceless Bullywug Shadow
  - "1": Faceless Bullywug Shadow
```

### Tiamat's Taunts

As Logan strikes down the faceless forms of the creatures he just fought in the real world, Tiamat's voice echoes from the purple sky, layered and heavy.

> [!quote] **The Dragon Queen Whispers**
> *"You slaughtered my servants in the mud, mortal. You thought it a victory? They were weak. Slain to weed out the chaff. Do you think your flesh is any more durable?"*

## Phase 2: The Obsidian Mirror of Waterdeep

### The Transition (Skill Challenge)

The moment the last bog minion disperses, the swamp water instantly freezes solid into black, polished glass. Logan must navigate a shifting landscape as the environment mutates around him.

> [!info] **Skill Challenge: The Reorienting Mind**
> The water beneath your feet hardens into smooth, black obsidian. Towering walls of dark, reflective stone erupt from the ice, twisting and locking together to form the familiar streets of Waterdeep, but everything is carved from flawless, cracked black glass. 
> 
> * **Athletics/Acrobatics (DC 13):** To keep his footing as the streets violently tilt and form under him. 
> * *Failure:* Logan falls prone, taking 1d6 psychic damage as a jagged spire of glass grazes his arm. The Black Dragon Mask grows noticeably heavier on his face.

### Encounter: The Cult Patrol

> [!danger] **The Streets of Glass**
> The obsidian buildings reflect your image a thousand times over, but in every reflection, the Black Dragon Mask is fused seamlessly into your flesh. From the alleyways of this ghost-city, faceless shadow forms wearing the robes of Cultist Dragonclaws and Initiates glide toward you, their hands ending in jagged blades of shadow.

* **Enemies:** 8x **Faceless Cultist Shadows**
* **Mechanics:** These shadows move faster, attempting to encircle Logan. Like Phase 1, they disperse in **1–2 hits**. 
* **The Twist:** When a cult shadow is popped, it releases a localized hiss of acid. Logan must succeed on a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or take 2 acid damage as the shadow dissolves.

```encounter
name: 3.5 Tiamat’s Trial - 2 (three rounds)
players:
  - Logan
creatures:
  - "1": Faceless Cultist Shadow
  - "1": Faceless Cultist Shadow
```

### Tiamat's Taunts

> [!quote] **The Dragon Queen Whispers**
> *"Look upon the city where you stole my treasure. You think you protect its fragile peace? Look closer at the glass, Logan. Look at what you are becoming. You wear my face. You harbor my malice. You belong to me."*

## Phase 3: The Shadow Lair of Tiamat

### The Descent

The obsidian streets of Waterdeep shatter like glass, dropping Logan into the abyss.

> [!danger] **The Nine Hells**
> The glass city bursts into a million flying shards. You plummet downward, crashing onto a jagged platform of black stone surrounded by an immense, subterranean cavern. This is a place of absolute nightmare. Towering pillars made of fused dragon bones stretch into a sky of roiling purple smoke. Pools of boiling, green acid hiss violently all around you. You are standing in the heart of the Shadow Lair of Tiamat.

### Encounter: The Dragon Wardens

Before the Queen manifests, her personal guard blocks the path.
* **Enemies:** 4x **Faceless Dragonkind Shadows** (Shifting, faceless shapes of Guard Drakes and Wyrmlings).
* **Mechanics:** These shadows require **2 solid hits** to disperse. They attempt to knock Logan prone or pin him down to the black stone platform.

```encounter
name: 3.5 Tiamat’s Trial - 3 (two rounds)
players:
  - Logan
creatures:
  - "1": Faceless Dragonkind Shadow
  - "1": Faceless Dragonkind Shadow
```

### The Climax: Shadow Tiamat Manifests

Once the dragon wardens are dispersed, the boiling pools of acid erupt violently. 

> [!danger] **The Five-Headed Shadow**
> The cavern shakes. The roiling purple smoke descends, condensing into a colossal, towering silhouette. Five massive, draconic heads snake out of the darkness; one white, one blue, one green, one red, and a central head of pure, abyssal black. Shadow Tiamat looms over you, her eyes glowing like dying stars. 
> 
> **"KNEEL, MORTAL."**

### The Confrontation Mechanics

1. **The Breath of Shadow:** Shadow Tiamat unveils a combined breath weapon of dark energy. Logan must make a **DC 14 Constitution saving throw**. 
   * *Success:* He stands his ground, taking minimal psychic damage. 
   * *Failure:* He is brought to his knees, feeling his life force drain.
1. **The Ultimate Offer:** Tiamat lowers her massive black head until her snout is inches from Logan's face. 

> [!quote] Tiamat
> *"You have cleared my gauntlet. You have broken my failed servants. You possess the spark of a true conqueror. Rezmir is a fool; Borngray is a child. I offer you real power, Logan. Accept the mask. Bow to your Queen, and I will grant you the strength to tear down Castle Naerytar."*

### The Choice

* **If Logan Defies Her:** He uses his action to strike at her shadow form. He automatically hits. His weapon cuts through the shadow, causing her to let out a reality-shattering roar of fury as the dream fractures.
* **If Logan Accepts/Hesitates:** The purple light of the mask flares with blinding warmth, seeping deep into his veins before the dream violently snaps shut.

## The Awakening: The Snap Back

> [!success] **Back to Reality**
> Shadow Tiamat's roar transitions instantly into the sound of a sputtering torch. 
> 
> Your foot hits the solid wood of the stairs leading up to Elf-lord Borngray's quarters. The heavy, cold air of the mud caves fills your lungs. You look to your left and right—your party members are right there beside you, weapons drawn, completely oblivious. To them, you didn't even blink. No time has passed. 
> 
> But beneath your armor, your chest is heaving, and your heart is hammering against your ribs like a trapped bird.

## Post-Session Notes

> [!tip] **The Lingering Shadow**
> * **If you defied Tiamat:** You feel an intense, cold resolve. You’re given the blessing of Bahamut, you gain **Persistent Inspiration** (once per long rest, you can reroll a d20 of your choosing. You also gain the use of a **Radiant Breath Weapon** (30-foot cone, 4d6 necrotic damage, DC 13 Dex half) once per long rest. For the next 24 hours, you have Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened by draconic creatures.
> * **If you accepted her offer/hesitated:** The Black Dragon Mask feels unnaturally warm against your face. You feel a dark reservoir of energy hidden inside you. You gain a one-time use of a **Shadow Breath Weapon** (30-foot cone, 4d6 necrotic damage, DC 13 Dex half). However, you feel an uncharacteristic urge to hoard the gold found in Borngray's tower, and your eyes briefly flash purple when you look into a mirror.

# Session Summary 

#### Campaign Interlude: The Trial of the Five-Headed Queen

The moment the bullywug shaman fell in the mud caves, Logan took his first step toward the stairs to hunt down Elf-lord Borngray, only for the Black Dragon Mask to violently lock onto his skull and plunge his mind into a freezing, silent void.  While time completely froze for his oblivious companions, Logan was forced to fight his way through a shifting, psychological gauntlet. He battled faceless shadow forms of lizardfolk and bullywugs in an infinite, sulfurous bog, navigated a twisted, black-glass mirror version of Waterdeep while being hunted by acidic cultist shadows, and finally descended into a subterranean abyss of dragon bones and boiling acid. 

At the heart of the shadow lair, the colossal, five-headed entity of Shadow Tiamat manifested to deliver an intoxicating ultimatum: accept the mask, bow to her, and receive the absolute power necessary to conquer the world. Though deeply torn by the temptation of the Dragon Queen, Logan chose ultimate defiance and struck directly through her shadow form, shattering the dream world with a reality-cracking roar. He instantly snapped back to reality with his foot hitting the first wooden step of the stairs, his heart hammering and his mask burning with a lingering chill—entirely changed, while his party members remained completely unaware that a war for his soul had just occurred in a single blink of an eye. 

#### Rewards Unlocked: Blessing of Bahamut

Because Logan stood resolute and defied the shadow of the Dragon Queen, he has been granted. 
* **Persistent Inspiration:** Once per long rest, you can reroll a d20 of your choosing. 
* **Radiant Breath Weapon:** Once per long rest, you can unleash a 30-foot cone of pure, blinding light (4d6 radiant damage, DC 13 Dex half). 
* **Draconic Resolve:** You have Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened by draconic creatures for the next 24 hours.
