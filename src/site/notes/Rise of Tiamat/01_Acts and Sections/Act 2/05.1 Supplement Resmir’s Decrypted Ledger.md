>As the arcane shorthand clicks into place, the messy runes resolve into a precise architectural diagram of the lodge below, complete with patrol schedules and security notes penned in Resmir’s sharp handwriting. Here is what you learn:

# Structure Overview: 

The lodge is a massive, two-story manor built from dark mountain timber and slate, nestled against the cliffside.

## Ground Floor (The Front Gate):

- Defenses: The main entrance consists of heavy oak double doors. There are two perimeter sentries stationed outside.
- Interior: Just inside the doors is a central barracks and guardroom. Resmir notes that a five-man quick-reaction force (a Captain and four veterans) is always stationed here, keeping warm by a massive hearth.
- The Patrol Schedule: Outer perimeter patrols rotate every two hours. A group wearing cult winter cloaks mimicking a returning patrol could potentially bluff their way right through the front doors.

## Second Floor (The Upper Levels):

- The Balcony: An exposed wooden balcony wraps around the second floor, directly above the main doors.
- The Vulnerability: Resmir has circled a specific window on the balcony that leads into the "Grand Trophy Hall," noting that the iron latch is frozen broken. Scaling the exterior timber frame provides direct access to the upper floor, completely bypassing the ground-floor barracks.
- A Final Warning: A hasty, bitter scrawl at the bottom of the page reads: "Talis remains secluded in her master suite on the second floor. She keeps her mindless armor constructs in the trophy hall. Do not disturb her unless Parnast is burning."
