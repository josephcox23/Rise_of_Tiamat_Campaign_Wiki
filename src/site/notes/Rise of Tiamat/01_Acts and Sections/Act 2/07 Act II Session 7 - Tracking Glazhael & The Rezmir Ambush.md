## Recap

> [!read-aloud] When last we left off...
> Frost and ozone filled Talis the White's opulent parlor as the high-ranking cultist and her guards fell to your relentless assault, leaving Robert to claim a crackling pair of glacial plate armor. 
> * A brief, chilling encounter with an invisible shadow stalker reminded you that Resmir’s secret police were still watching from the freezing sidelines.
> What followed was a thorough scouring of the sanctuary:
> * Jacob uncovered secrets hidden on the map on Talis’s desk pointing away from Parnast and straight toward the perilous heights of Glazhael’s iceberg lair.
> * Andy secured a vital cache of magical arrows and a ring of cold resistance along with heavy trade bars, ensuring the party was ready for the brutal alpine climb.
> * Logan, initially failed at reading the archived documents in his polar bear form, but wild shaped back into his Dragonborn form to discover the crucial banner passwords needed to slip past future cult checkpoints. 
>
> With Talis defeated and new intelligence in hand, you turned your backs on the hunting lodge, steeling yourselves for a vertical ascent into the high Greypeaks. 
> Now, as the biting wind howls across the sheer ice cliffs and your breath freezes in the thin air, the true race to claim the White Dragon Mask begins...

## Scene 1: Skill Challenge – The Ascent to the Iceberg

> [!read-aloud]
> Following Talis's map, you leave the timber line far below, entering a deathly quiet alpine zone where the wind screams across sheer blue ice walls. The path narrows to a foot-wide ledge above a bottomless gorge. To reach the glacier, you’ll need to coordinate your footing, read the unstable weather patterns, and manage your gear against the biting frost.
