# Session 5 - The Frozen Chase & The White Mask

## Scene 1: Arrival in the Greypeak Mountains

> The blinding blue light of the teleportation circle snaps out instantly. The muggy, sulfurous heat of the Mere vanishes, replaced in a heartbeat by a howling, bone-chilling wind that carries sheets of driving snow. 
> You stand on an ancient stone portal platform built into a high, jagged ridge in the icy Greypeak Mountains. The air here is thin, biting, and freezing. Below you, half-buried in the white expanse, a stone pathway winds through the pines toward a grand, multi-story timber lodge tucked into the mountainside.

> [!warning] Environmental Hazard: High Altitude & Extreme Cold
> * **Cold Exposure:** Any character not wearing cold weather gear or using magical protection must succeed on a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** at the start of every hour or suffer 1 level of Exhaustion. 
> * **Movement:** Uncleared snow drifts count as difficult terrain.

> You barely have time to process the freezing air before the crunch of boots on snow breaks the howling wind. Emerging from the whiteout are two heavily armored cultists, their halberds at the ready. Snarling at their sides are two Ambush Drakes, their scales rimed with frost. One of the guards reaches for a brass signal horn at his belt. Roll for initiative!

```encounter
name: Portal Platform Sentries

  - John (Jacob)
  - Legolas (Andy)
  - Logan
  - Kana Skysmasher (Robert)

  - "2": Cult Veteran
```

> [!information] Tactical Notes: The Signal Horn
> * **The Horn** (Round 2, Init 10): A Cult Veteran attempts to sound a brass signal horn. If sounded, double the guards at the main gate.
> * **Secondary Objective**: Collapsing Arcane Arch (Round 3): Residual portal energy is destabilizing the stone archway. On Round 3, three massive ice-covered stones collapse across the platform. Players must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d10 bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.
> * **Unseen Stalker Clue**: A successful DC 14 Perception/Investigation check mid-fight reveals fresh, light footprints in the snow leading off the platform edge onto sheer cliff face, confirming the stalker followed them through the portal.

## Scene 1.5: Skill Challenge - The Frostfell Ridge Descent

> The portal platform offers no shelter, and the only way forward is a steep, treacherous trail winding down the jagged mountain spine. The powder is knee-deep, masking hidden crevasses, and the wind threatens to pull you right off the cliffside. Worse still, you can't shake the chilling sensation that something unseen is pacing you in the whiteout. You'll need to work together to navigate this descent safely.

> [!Info] After two successes:
> ### Read-Aloud: Eyes in the Whiteout
> As you pause to catch your breath against a howling gust of wind, someone notices a brief disruption in the fresh snow a few yards off the path—as if heavy boots had stepped there, but no one is standing in sight. A heartbeat later, the sharp snap of an icicle breaking echoes just behind your shoulder. When you whip around, there is only a swirling vortex of blinding white powder and an abrupt, unnatural drop in the air temperature.

## Scene 2: The Greypeak Hunting Lodge Approach

> Pushing through the worst of the blizzard, you finally find a cluster of dense, ancient pines that break the biting wind. It’s a temporary reprieve, but enough cover to catch your breath, you light a small, smokeless fire, and thaw your frozen hands. Through the treeline below, you can just make out the dark timber structure of the cult's hunting lodge. Now is the time to gather your strength and figure out exactly what you're walking into. 

> Even huddled close to the smoky pine fire, a sudden, piercing draft cuts through your cloaks, frosting the condensation on your breath into jagged patterns. Glancing back at the fresh trail you just carved through the snow leading down the ridge, you see a faint, translucent indentation—like a handprint pressed into a snowbank—slowly fading away as the wind sweeps it clean. Whatever followed you through the portal from the Mere isn't just tracking you; it’s closing in.

## Scene 3: The Greypeak Hunting Lodge Approach

### Read-Aloud: Route 2 - The Brazen Bluff (Front Gates)

> ***(Requires DC 14 Deception check to bypass guards and enter Scene 4)***
> Pulling your heavy cult cloaks tight around your shoulders, you step out of the treeline and onto the main path. The wind howls, throwing powder into the air and obscuring your faces as you march in formation toward the heavy oak double doors. Up ahead, the two perimeter sentries clutch their halberds, squinting through the whiteout at your approach. You have the uniforms, and you know the patrol schedule. It's time to see how good your acting really is.
