## Scene 6: Talis the White’s Sanctuary

> Heavy double doors open into an opulent suite draped in polar bear furs, silver embroideries, and icy crystal sconces. Standing at a balcony overlook with a silver goblet in hand is a high-ranking cultist wearing white plate armor and an icy silk mantle, Talis the White. Two heavy-armored bodyguards flank her, swords drawn, but she raises a gloved hand to stay their blades.
> "I know who you are," she says, her voice smooth and cold as glass. "You broke Borngray's hold and pursued Resmir. But Resmir is a fool, and Varram is a thief who stole the White Dragon Mask that was rightfully mine. Perhaps we can come to an... arrangement."

## Session Summary

- Combat session, the party entered the parlor of Talis the White and were given the opportunity to form an alliance if they agreed to retrieve the White Dragon Mask from Varram the White her rival for the White Wyrmspeaker position 
- Jacob failed the persuasion check allowing Talis to see through their ruse (the plan was to agree and betray her later). 
- Talis along with her two guards attacked, putting up a great fight. Halfway through Talis turned the floor to ice and summoned two Ice Mephits (which she dispatched with an Ice Storm by mistake)
- Once Talis and her minions were defeated, Robert obtained her armor (+1 Scale Mail with a reaction:

> name: Glacial Shield

    desc: "When hit by a melee attack, Talis can use her reaction to cause a burst of sub-zero frost to erupt from her armor. The attacker takes 9 (2d8) cold damage."

- Then the shadow stalker appeared and was quickly dismissed without any issue
- The party investigated the parlor and discovered:
	- a secret on the map on Talis’ desk detailing a hidden trail to Parnast which will allow them to arrive before Skyreach Castle’s departure window. 
	- A ring of cold resistance and 400g 
	- The password for the sky-castle banner signals: “Hail Tiamat’s Might” which will allow them to pass through the checkpoints in the castle
	- Cold weather gear for all members
	- 20x +1 Magic Arrows
	- 220g, 500sp
	- A potion of healing
	- A Blood-Inscribed Onyx Amulet (Cukt Secret Police lore clue)
