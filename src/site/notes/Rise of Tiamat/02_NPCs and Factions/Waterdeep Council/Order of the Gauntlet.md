# Order of the Gauntlet

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *" Vigilance is our shield, and righteous striking is our sword against the creeping shadows of tyranny."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Order believes that evil must be met with swift, open justice and unwavering moral clarity. They hold that waiting for evil to strike first is a tactical failure, and that righteousness requires active protection of the innocent and punishing wrongdoers openly.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by everyday citizens and frontier settlements as honorable saviors and staunch protectors who arrive to shield the vulnerable from monsters and tyrants.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Members wear polished silver armor, immaculate tabards, and gauntlet insignia bearing the sacred symbols of deities like Tyr, Helm, and Torm.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Eradicate wicked organizations like the Cult of the Dragon, defend the innocent from chromatic dragon depredations, and uphold justice across the Sword Coast.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Deploying paladin strike forces and knightly chapters to secure frontier towns, patrol high-risk travel routes, and reinforce allied strongholds against cult raids.
  * Providing moral support, tactical leadership, and faction boons (such as the Lion's Battle Horn) to trusted champions.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Disciplined military squads, holy crusaders, divine spellcasters, front-line armored phalanxes, and righteous zeal backed by coalition support.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A structured, knightly hierarchy governed by high-ranking preceptors, paladin commanders, and senior marshals who answer to regional high commands.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Sir Isteval\|Sir Isteval]]:** *Knight-Commander & Council Representative* — A righteous, disabled paladin of Cormyr who serves as the moral anchor of the Order and a vocal champion on the Council of Waterdeep.
  * **Local Chapter Preceptors:** Regional commanders managing local chapter houses, training novitiates, and coordinating front-line defenses.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** [[Rise of Tiamat/03_Locations/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]] (Order Chapter House) — A secure administrative and spiritual base within the City of Splendors.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Frontline chapter houses, fortified strongholds, and frontier garrisons spanning from Daggerford and the North down to the Sword Coast trade lanes.
