# Connerad Brawnanvil

*The proud, battle-hardened King of Mithral Hall who brings dwarven steel and unyielding stubbornness to the Council's defense coalition.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Shield Dwarf, Advanced
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Gravelly, booming, and fiercely direct; punctuates his arguments with sharp gestures of a scarred warhammer.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To defend dwarven strongholds, forge unbreakable tactical alliances against the chromatic menace, and avenge comrades lost to dragonfire.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **[[Mithral Hall\|Mithral Hall]]:** His subterranean mountain kingdom, home to the finest smiths and heaviest infantry in the North.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** A prominent voice demanding heavily armored frontlines and superior weaponry for the allied armies.
- **The Party:** Respected champions whose exploits in recovering artifact masks earn his gruff approval and access to dwarven forgecraft.
