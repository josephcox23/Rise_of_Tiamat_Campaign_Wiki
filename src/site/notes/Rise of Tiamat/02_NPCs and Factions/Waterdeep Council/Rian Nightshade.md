# Rian Nightshade

*The sharp, pragmatic Zhentarim operative who handles information brokering and covert logistics for the Black Network.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** She/Her
> - **Race/Age:** Human, Early 30s
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Crisp, transactional, and observant; she misses nothing and measures every interaction in terms of leverage and profit.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To position the Zhentarim as an indispensable power broker in the North while securing profitable trade routes and eliminating cult interference in black-market supply chains.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **Zhentarim (The Black Network):** Her criminal and mercenary syndicate, which provides covert caches, poison supplies, and cutthroats.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** An uneasy alliance born of necessity; she attends council meetings to keep tabs on the lords and offer pragmatic, morally gray solutions.
- **The Party:** Potential business partners who can be hired or bartered with for high-risk sabotage missions.
