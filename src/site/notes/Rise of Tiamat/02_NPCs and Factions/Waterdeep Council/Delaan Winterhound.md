# Delaan Winterhound

*The rugged, steadfast ranger representing the Emerald Enclave who coordinates wilderness survival and tracks cult movements across the North.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Human, Late 40s
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Calm, weathered, and speaks with the measured patience of someone who spends weeks alone in the deep wilderness.

---

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To protect the natural balance of the Sword Coast and ensure the Cult of the Dragon's despoliation of the land and wildlife is completely halted.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **[[02_NPCs and Factions/Emerald Enclave\|Emerald Enclave]]:** His primary allegiance; he mobilizes druids and rangers to monitor wilderness trails, swamps, and mountain passes.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** Sits as the Enclave representative, though he is often uncomfortable with the heavy politicking of city lords.
- **The Party:** Allies in the natural world who rely on Enclave maps, balms, and wilderness intelligence.
