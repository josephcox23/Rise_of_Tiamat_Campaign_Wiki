# Council of Waterdeep

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"In the unity of the Sword Coast lies our only shield against the shadow of the Dragon Queen."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Council believes that the sovereign city-states, factions, and free peoples of the North cannot survive the Cult of the Dragon individually. They champion coalition, collective defense, and decisive strike missions over passive diplomacy or political bickering.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by everyday citizens of Waterdeep and the surrounding realms as the ultimate bastion of hope and authority—the supreme governing body coordinating the defense of civilization against the rising cult threat.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Envoys, guards, and agents often wear the silver and blue heraldry of Waterdeep, carry official Lords' Alliance credentials, or bear high-profile patronage tokens like **Silver Warding Tokens** granted directly to trusted champions.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Unite the fragmented powers of the Sword Coast—including the major factions and ancient chromatic/metallic dragon alliances—to assemble a grand army, dismantle the Cult's leadership, and halt Tiamat's summoning at the Well of Dragons.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Directing the party on high-excitement strike missions to track down the stolen Dragon Masks and dismantle cult strongholds (such as Castle Naerytar, Glazhael's iceberg lair, and Skyreach Castle).
  * Equipping champions with faction boons, magical gear, and critical logistical intelligence.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Diplomatic coalition-building, funding high-level adventurers as elite strike teams, mobilizing City Watch forces and Lords' Alliance armies, and coordinating clandestine intelligence sharing across major spy networks.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** An emergency coalition council composed of high-ranking representatives from the major Sword Coast factions (Lords' Alliance, Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, Emerald Enclave, and Zhentarim), presided over by the Open Lord of Waterdeep.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** [[Rise of Tiamat/03_Locations/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]] — The glittering City of Splendors, serving as the political hub where the Council convenes to review the party's progress and issue new directives.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Safehouses, embassy villas, and regional strongholds spanning the Sword Coast from the High Road and the Mere of Dead Men up to the Greypeak Mountains and Parnast.
