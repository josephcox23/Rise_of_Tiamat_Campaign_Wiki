# King Melandrach

*The weary, aristocratic monarch of the Misty Forest elves who struggles to balance the safety of his woodland realm with the wider crisis of the Sword Coast.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Wood Elf, Ancient
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Melancholic, measured, and cautious; carries a heavy burden of leadership and private family grief.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To keep the Misty Forest secure from cult incursions and preserve elven lives, avoiding reckless foreign wars whenever possible.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **[[Elven Sovereignty\|Elven Sovereignty]]:** His woodland kingdom, guarding ancient shrines and secret groves like the resting place of *Lyraeli’s Lament*.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** A reluctant participant who prefers isolationism until forced to confront the existential threat of Tiamat.
- **The Party:** Adventurers who will inevitably uncover his son's betrayal when they reach Skyreach Castle in the Misty Forest.
