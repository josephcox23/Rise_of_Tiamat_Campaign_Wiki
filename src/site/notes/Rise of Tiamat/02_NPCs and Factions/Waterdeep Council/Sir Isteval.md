# Sir Isteval

*The righteous, disabled paladin-knight of Cormyr who fights with unwavering moral clarity and champions the downtrodden across the Sword Coast.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Human, Late 50s
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Earnest, warm, and resolute; walks with a heavy limp supported by an ornate brace, but speaks with the ringing conviction of a true hero.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To restore justice, protect innocent folk from cult atrocities, and inspire courage in a fractured land through deeds of selfless honor.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Order of the Gauntlet\|Order of the Gauntlet]]:** His knightly order, embodying righteous zeal and frontline protection against tyranny.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** A moral anchor on the Council who constantly reminds the lords of their duty to ordinary citizens.
- **The Party:** Fellow champions of justice whom he regards with deep fondness and admiration.
