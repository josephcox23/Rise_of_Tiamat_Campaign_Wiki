# Taern Hornblade

*The venerable High Mage of Silverymoon, wielding formidable arcane power and deep historical wisdom in service of the Council.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Human, Ancient (Elongated life via magic)
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Soft-spoken, deeply intellectual, and calm; radiates an aura of quiet, immense magical potency.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> To preserve magical knowledge, protect the northern city-states, and unravel the arcane mechanics of the Cult's planar summoning rituals.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **Silverymoon:** His home city of learning and magic, which he represents on the Council.
- **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/The Council of Waterdeep\|The Council of Waterdeep]]:** Works closely with Lady Laeral Silverhand on magical countermeasures and intelligence regarding the Dragon Masks.
- **The Party:** Champions who retrieve volatile magical artifacts (like the Dragon Masks) that require high-level containment and study.
