# Flaming Fist

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"We are the iron wall that protects Baldur’s Gate, and our terms are absolute."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Flaming Fist operates as a powerful mercenary army and official military force, believing that absolute order and heavy defensive readiness are the only true shields against chaos, monsters, and war.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by citizens and merchants as a stern, formidable military machine that maintains strict security, though their mercenary origins mean everything has a price.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Heavy steel plate armor, crimson tabards bearing the blazing fist insignia, and disciplined formation marching.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Protect Baldur's Gate, maintain trade route security across the Sword Coast, and eliminate external threats to regional stability.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Enforcing martial law and border checks around Baldur's Gate.
  * Coordinating heavy infantry support and coalition troop deployments for the Council of Waterdeep.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Large-scale military phalanxes, heavy cavalry, seasoned mercenary captains, fortified garrisons, and rigorous weapon and armor manufacturing.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** Commanded by a Grand Duke and Marshal who oversees high-ranking officers, centurions, and mercenary squads.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Uldar Ravengard\|Uldar Ravengard]]:** *Grand Duke & Marshal of the Flaming Fist* — Supreme military leader directing the organization and its coalition efforts.
  * **Mercenary Centurions:** Field officers managing frontline patrols and garrison defenses.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** [[Rise of Tiamat/03_Locations/Baldur's Gate\|Baldur's Gate]] (The High Hall) — The military command center overlooking the metropolis.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Border watchtowers, fortified encampments, and regional security checkpoints spanning the Chionthar River valley.
