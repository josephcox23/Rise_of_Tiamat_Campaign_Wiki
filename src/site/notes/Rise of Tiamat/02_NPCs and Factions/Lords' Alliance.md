# Lords' Alliance

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"In mutual defense and sovereign cooperation lies the survival of civilization against the dark."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Lords' Alliance is a coalition of rulers from independent cities and towns across the North who believe that cooperation, collective military strength, and diplomatic unity are essential to protect civilized lands from external threats.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by citizens of member states as a powerful guarantor of security and economic stability, though smaller frontier towns occasionally view alliance lords as distant and politically heavy-handed.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Envoys, diplomats, and military commanders wear official alliance heraldry, carry credentials stamped with the seals of member city-states, and march alongside disciplined city watch and regular armies.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Unite the sovereign powers of the North to counter existential threats like the Cult of the Dragon, maintain secure trade lanes, and preserve political stability.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Coordinating multi-city military deployments and funding specialized adventuring strike teams to dismantle cult strongholds.
  * Pooling intelligence and resources through the Council of Waterdeep.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Regular standing armies, city guards, formal diplomacy, political alliances, mercenary contracts, and coordinated regional defenses.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A ruling council composed of rulers and representatives from major northern city-states (including Waterdeep, Baldur's Gate, Silverymoon, and Neverwinter).
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Laeral Silverhand\|Laeral Silverhand]]:** *Open Lord of Waterdeep* — A leading voice and political anchor for the alliance.
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Uldar Ravengard\|Uldar Ravengard]]:** *Grand Duke of Baldur's Gate* — Commander of alliance military forces and the Flaming Fist.
  * **City Lords & Envoys:** Rulers and delegates representing member city-states on the coalition council.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** [[Rise of Tiamat/03_Locations/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]] & Member Capitals — Governing centers across major allied cities of the North.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Border garrisons, fortified watchtowers, and regional command posts spanning Sword Coast trade routes.
