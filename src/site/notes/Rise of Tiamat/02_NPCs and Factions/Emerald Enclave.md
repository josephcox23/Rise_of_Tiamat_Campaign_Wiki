# Emerald Enclave

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"The wilderness sustains all life, and we are its shield against unnatural corruption and destruction."*

* **Core Philosophy:** The Emerald Enclave believes in maintaining the delicate balance of nature. They hold that civilization and wilderness must coexist in harmony, and that any force—such as the Cult of the Dragon or apocalyptic dragon gods—that threatens to upset the natural order must be ruthlessly pruned back.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by rural folk and woodsmen as wise protectors and herbalists, while urban politicians occasionally view them as reclusive, untrustworthy isolationists.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Members wear practical, weather-worn leathers and furs colored in rich forest greens and browns, marked with leaf or animal insignia and carrying natural spellcasting focuses.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Protect the natural world from the catastrophic devastation threatened by the Cult of the Dragon and chromatic dragons, preserving the balance of wild ecosystems.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Monitoring wilderness corridors, animal migrations, and swamp approaches (such as the Mere of Dead Men and Greypeak foothills).
  * Equipping champions with wilderness maps, herbal balms, and tracking intelligence.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Druidic magic, ranger scouting networks, wild shaping, wilderness survival tactics, and the mobilization of woodland creatures and beast allies.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A decentralized, druidic network of high-ranking circles and senior wardens who coordinate regional protection without heavy bureaucratic hierarchy.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Delaan Winterhound\|Delaan Winterhound]]:** *Ranger Warden & Council Representative* — A rugged, steadfast ranger representing the Enclave on the Council of Waterdeep, coordinating wilderness survival and tracking cult movements across the North.
  * **Regional Circle Druids:** Wardens who manage local groves, wildlife corridors, and natural sanctuaries.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** The Wilderness / Wild Groves — Operating across untamed regions, forests, and remote green spaces throughout the Sword Coast.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Hidden woodland groves, ranger outposts, and monitoring stations spanning from the Mere of Dead Men up to the northern mountain passes.
