# The Lords' Alliance

##  Overview

A coalition of rulers from various northern settlements dedicated to maintaining order, security, and economic stability along the Sword Coast. Under the leadership of Open Lord **Lady Laeral Silverhand**, they serve as the primary operational backing for the party's current deployment.

## Faction Benefits & Assets

* **Silver Warding Tokens:** Provided directly to the party for magical defense during their trek north.
* **Political Leverage:** High authority when dealing with local guards, military outposts, and official trade networks across Alliance territories.
