# The Harpers

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"We walk in the shadows so that others may live in the light."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Harpers believe that tyranny and injustice must be opposed through covert intelligence, espionage, and subtle intervention. They strive to keep secrets out of the hands of the wicked and ensure that no single power dominates the Sword Coast.
* **Public Perception:** Regarded by ordinary folk as mysterious, benevolent myth-figures or wandering bards, while corrupt nobles and tyrants view them with paranoid dread.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Operatives typically operate in civilian disguise, carrying subtle tokens such as a silver pin shaped like a harp and moon, or communicating via hidden musical ciphers and covert passphrases.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Uncover the Cult of the Dragon's secrets, track the stolen Dragon Masks, and dismantle their apocalyptic conspiracy from the shadows before total war erupts.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Infiltrating cult strongholds, intercepting encrypted dispatches, and tracking Wyrmspeaker movements across the Sword Coast.
  * Supplying trusted agents with specialized intelligence, magical tools, and faction boons (such as Cipher Rings and Dust of Disappearance).
* **Tactics & Resources:** Espionage, information brokering, covert infiltration, illusion magic, safehouse networks, and clandestine sabotage.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A decentralized, cell-based network of semi-autonomous agents and master bards guided by the High Harpers council, operating largely through trust and individual initiative.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **Harper Spymasters & Bard Agents:** Regional contacts who provide covert assignments, ciphers, and safehouse locations to trusted adventurers.
  * **Informants & Scholars:** Operatives embedded within major cities and scholarly institutions tracking arcane anomalies.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** Decentralized Safehouses — Operating out of hidden cellars, tavern backrooms, and secret libraries across major Sword Coast settlements like Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Traveling bard troupes, hidden relay stations, and covert listening posts spanning trade routes and frontier towns.
