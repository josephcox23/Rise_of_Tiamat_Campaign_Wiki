# Zhentarim

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"Trade follows the dagger, and profit belongs to those smart enough to seize it."*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** The Zhentarim—known widely as the Black Network—operate on pure pragmatism, wealth acquisition, and shadow influence. They believe that power belongs to those who control trade routes, information networks, and mercenary supply chains, regardless of who sits on the throne.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed by everyday citizens and law-abiding merchants with deep suspicion and fear as a ruthless criminal syndicate, though in times of war they are often treated as a necessary, chillingly efficient evil.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Operatives often wear dark, practical attire, carry discreet daggers or hidden insignia, and utilize coded cyphers, shadow magic, and covert black-market caches.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Expand the Black Network's commercial and political monopolies across the Sword Coast, positioning themselves to profit from the chaos while neutralizing any faction that threatens their economic dominance.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Infiltrating conflict zones and trade lanes to secure high-value contraband, poison supplies, and intelligence.
  * Maintaining an uneasy, transactional alliance with the Council of Waterdeep while secretly hedging their bets and monitoring cult movements.
* **Tactics & Resources:** Underground smuggling rings, cutthroat mercenaries, information brokering, poison caches, blackmail, and ruthless economic leverage.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A ruthless, cell-based network governed by shadow lords and high-ranking operatives like Rian Nightshade who manage regional contracts and profit margins.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * **[[Rise of Tiamat/02_NPCs and Factions/Waterdeep Council/Rian Nightshade\|Rian Nightshade]]:** *Zhentarim Operative & Information Broker* — A sharp, pragmatic fixer handling black-market logistics and intelligence for the Black Network.
  * **Regional Fixers & Mercenary Captains:** Local commanders who oversee safehouses, contraband stashes, and cutthroat operatives.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** Darkhold — A formidable mountaintop fortress serving as the central stronghold of the Black Network.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** Smuggling dens, hidden safehouses, and shadow caches spanning the Sword Coast from Waterdeep up through the high road and swamp borders.
