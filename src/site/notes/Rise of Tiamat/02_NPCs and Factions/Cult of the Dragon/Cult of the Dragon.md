# Cult of the Dragon

> [!quote] Motto / Creed
> *"Hail Tiamat’s Might! Let the skies burn and the old empires crumble beneath the five-headed Queen of Dragons!"*

## Overview & Ideology

* **Core Philosophy:** Under the reformed leadership of Supreme Leader Severin, the Cult has abandoned its old obsession with undead dracoliches. They now believe that living chromatic dragons are the rightful rulers of the world and that summoning Tiamat—the five-headed Dragon Queen—from the Nine Hells will usher in an apocalyptic age of draconic supremacy.
* **Public Perception:** Viewed across the Sword Coast as ruthless, fanatical raiders and existential terrorists. To everyday townsfolk and noble lords alike, their sudden emergence from the shadows signals impending pillage, dragonfire, and ruin.
* **Visual Identifiers:** Members wear dark purple and black robes or scale-trimmed armor emblazoned with the five-clawed talon or multi-headed dragon iconography of Tiamat. Elite Dragonwings wear stylized winged cloaks and draconic masks, while high-ranking Wyrmspeakers bear ceremonial regalia tied to their specific chromatic dragon color.

## Goals & Methods

* **Primary Objective:** Gather the five ancient **Dragon Masks** (Black, White, Green, Blue, and Red), amass a colossal hoard of gold and treasure from across the Sword Coast, and execute a massive blood-and-gold ritual at the **Well of Dragons** to tear open a planar portal and summon Tiamat into the mortal realm.
* **Short-Term Operations:** 
  * Desperately hunting down the party across the Sword Coast to reclaim the stolen **Black Mask**.
  * Mobilizing flying strongholds like **Skyreach Castle** from Parnast to transport stolen wealth and consolidate the remaining masks (**Green**, **Blue**, **Red**, and **White**).
* **Tactics & Resources:** The Cult relies on rapid, coordinated strikes combining fanatic infantry, winged Dragonwing shock troops, enslaved or coerced humanoid tribes (such as bullywugs and lizardfolk), mercenary veterans, elemental and chromatic dragon allies, and splinter factions of the Red Wizards of Thay.

## Hierarchy & Key Figures

* **Leadership Structure:** A rigid, dogmatic pyramid ruled by **Severin**, the Red Wyrmspeaker and Supreme Leader. Beneath him sit the four other **Wyrmspeakers**—each meant to wield one of the five Dragon Masks and command a chromatic wing of the Cult—supported by regional commanders, Dragonwings, and dark spellcasters.

## Territory & Strongholds

* **Primary Base:** [[Well of Dragons\|Well of Dragons]] — A massive, fortified volcanic caldera serving as the Cult's central temple, treasury vault, and ritual site for Tiamat's summoning.
* **Outposts & Fronts:** 
    * **Active / Contested:** **Skyreach Castle** (a colossal floating cloud-ship loading treasure in Parnast before heading toward the Misty Forest), a lightning-shattered desert canyon stronghold (Blue Mask), and a fiery volcanic cavern (Red Mask).
  * **Fallen / Compromised:** **Castle Naerytar** (overthrown in a lizardfolk rebellion), the **Greypeak Hunting Lodge** (cleared), and **Glazhael’s Iceberg Lair** (neutralized).
