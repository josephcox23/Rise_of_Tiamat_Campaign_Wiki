# Glazhael the Cloudchaser

*The majestic, terrifying ancient white dragon who rules the icy vaults of the Sea of Moving Ice and demands absolute proof of strength from those who would command him.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Ancient White Dragon, Centuries Old
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** A booming, sub-zero growl that rattles stalactites; moves with terrifying predatory grace and radiates chilling arrogance.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> Glazhael wants to hoard vast treasures of silver and ice, maintain his solitary dominance over his glacial territory, and defend his mountain domain—refusing to bow to mortal wars or fight on behalf of Tiamat's grand ambitions.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Varram the White:** The desperate Wyrmspeaker who occupied Glazhael's iceberg lair and attempting to force the White Dragon Mask into alignment.
- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Talis the White:** The Greypeaks cultist who monitored his lair from her alpine hunting lodge.
- **Tiamat:** The five-headed dragon goddess whom Glazhael views as a primordial force beyond mortal control, stubbornly refusing to participate in her summoning at the Well of Dragons.
