# Varram the White

*The desperate, power-hungry White Wyrmspeaker trying to force the White Dragon Mask into alignment beneath the gaze of an ancient white dragon.*

> [!abstract] Quick Profile
> - **Pronouns:** He/Him
> - **Race/Age:** Dwarf, Middle-aged
> - **Voice/Mannerism:** Booming yet strained with frantic urgency; sweat beads on his brow even in sub-zero cold as he chants over arcane rituals with white-knuckled desperation.

---

## Core Identity

> [!quote] Motivation
> **What do they want?** 
> Varram wanted to master the White Dragon Mask, bend Glazhael the Cloudchaser to his will, and solidify his rank as White Wyrmspeaker to prove his worth to Severin and silence rivals like Talis the White.

---

## Connections & Relationships

- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Glazhael the Cloudchaser:** The ancient white dragon whose iceberg lair Varram occupied; Varram desperately attempted to tame and command the beast using the mask's authority, only to find himself on the brink of being slain by the dragon.
- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Talis the White:** His bitter rival within the Cult who despised him for stealing her promotion and coveted the White Dragon Mask for herself.
- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Severin:** The Cult's Supreme Leader, whose wrath Varram feared if he failed to secure the mask and report on time.
- **06_Bestiary/Cult of the Dragon/Wyrmspeaker Rezmir:** Fellow Wyrmspeaker and Black Mask bearer; Varram held dispatch scrolls detailing Rezmir's mobilization of Skyreach Castle at Parnast.

---

## Roleplay & Interaction Notes

* **First Impression:** Frantic, fanatical, and cornered—found chanting furiously over a floating prismatic pedestal on a slick ice floor while an ancient white dragon looms above him.
* **What they know:** The Cult's broader troop movements, the impending launch of Skyreach Castle at Parnast, and the volatile arcane mechanics of the White Dragon Mask.
* **What they want from the party:** For them to stay back until his ritual completes—or, once interrupted, their immediate destruction via fierce clerical magic and defensive wards.
* **Story Connection:** Serves as the dual-boss climax alongside Glazhael in Act II (Session 6) inside the iceberg lair. Disrupting his ritual stuns him and inflicts psychic blowback, leading to his defeat and allowing the party to claim the White Dragon Mask and his dispatch scrolls.
